Maintaining automatic garage doors is a legal duty. In residential buildings they must be maintained and checked at least twice a year, with every visit recorded in a maintenance logbook. The same requirement applies to doors and gates in workplaces.

Code de la construction et de l'habitation, art. R. 134-58 · Code du travail, art. R. 4224-12

The engineer

Miguel, electronics engineer

Miguel holds a French electronics engineering degree and founded the company on a simple idea: a motorised gate is a machine, and a machine is looked after methodically. Every installation has its file, and every visit leaves a written record.

He does the work himself, on every brand, and remains your contact from the quote to the last visit. You can deal with him in French, Spanish or English.
